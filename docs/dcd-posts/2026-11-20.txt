Ever spend ten minutes hunting for that one photo of your insurance card or ID?

A simple fix is creating one folder in the Files app just for the documents you need most.

Open the Files app, tap to create a New Folder, and give it a clear name like “Important Docs.”

Then move a few key files or photos into it so they're grouped together instead of scattered.

Next time you need something in a hurry, you'll know exactly where to look.
