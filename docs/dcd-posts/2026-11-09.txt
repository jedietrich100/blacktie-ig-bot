⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Trying to tap something at the very top of a tall screen while holding your phone in one hand can feel like a stretch.

Reachability gently slides the whole screen down so everything sits closer to your thumb.

Go to Settings, then Accessibility, then Touch, and turn on Reachability.

Once it's on, swipe down along the bottom edge of the screen whenever you need things closer.

Tap what you need, then tap the empty space above to slide the screen back up.
