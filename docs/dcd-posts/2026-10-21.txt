Tired of your phone ringing for numbers you don't recognize?

Your iPhone has a setting that can quietly send unknown callers to voicemail, while calls from people in your contacts still ring through as usual.

This can help cut down on random calls, including ones from numbers pretending to be someone they're not.

To check it: open Settings, tap Phone, then look for Silence Unknown Callers and turn it on.

You can always turn it back off if you're expecting a call from a new number, like a doctor's office.
