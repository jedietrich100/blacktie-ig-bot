⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Do you ever scroll through hundreds of photos trying to find one receipt or ID picture?

A simple album can fix that for good.

Open the Photos app, tap Albums, then tap the plus sign to create a new album.

Name it something clear, like "Important" or "Documents," and add the photos that matter most.

From now on, new important photos can go straight into that album, so they're easy to find without scrolling.
