Typing passwords on a tiny screen is one of the most annoying parts of using a phone.

Your iPhone can remember many of your passwords for you, so you don't have to type them again.

When you see a username or password field, look for a small suggestion bar above the keyboard or a key icon in the field.

Tap it, choose the saved login, and confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.

This can save time and help you avoid retyping long, tricky passwords by hand.
