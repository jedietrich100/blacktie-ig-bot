⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Do you ever open an app and notice it's asking you to update, again?

You can let your iPhone handle that quietly in the background instead.

Go to Settings, then App Store, and turn on Automatic App Updates.

From then on, your apps can update on their own, usually overnight, so you spend less time tapping "update" and more time just using your phone.
