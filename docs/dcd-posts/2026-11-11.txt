⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Your email account is often the key to everything else — if someone gets into it, they can reset passwords for your other accounts too.

Two-step verification adds a second lock. Even if someone learns your password, they also need a code sent to your phone or email to get in.

Open your email app or account website, look in Settings for "Security" or "Sign-In," and look for an option like "Two-Step Verification" or "Two-Factor Authentication." Turn it on and follow the prompts.

It takes under a minute, but it can make your account much harder to break into.
