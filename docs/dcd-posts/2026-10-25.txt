⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Typing on a small keyboard can feel slow, especially for longer messages.

Your iPhone has a built-in way to speak instead of type, and it works in almost any text field.

Just tap into a message or note, then tap the small microphone icon on the keyboard. Speak naturally, then tap the keyboard icon again when you're done to switch back to typing.

It's a simple way to save time and reduce typing frustration, especially for longer texts or emails.
