⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

A message can show a friendly name like "Bank Support" even when the actual email address looks nothing like the real company.

Display names are easy to fake, but the full email address underneath is harder to disguise.

Next time an email feels slightly off, tap or click on the sender's name at the top of the message.

This reveals the full email address, so you can compare it to ones you've received from that company before.

If the address looks strange, misspelled, or unfamiliar, treat the message with extra caution before clicking anything inside it.

This small check takes seconds and can help you catch a fake message before it causes any trouble.
