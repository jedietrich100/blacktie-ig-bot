Does your phone buzz all night with notifications you don’t need until morning?

A Sleep Focus can quietly hold most alerts until you’re ready to see them.

Open Settings, tap Focus, choose Sleep, then set a schedule that matches your bedtime.

Your calls and messages are still there in the morning, just without the overnight noise.
