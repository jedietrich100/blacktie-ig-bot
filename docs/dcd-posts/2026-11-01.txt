⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Ever type something, make a mistake, and feel like you have to start all over?

Your iPhone has a quiet little safety net built right in.

Just swipe left with three fingers on the screen to undo your last typing action. Swipe right with three fingers to redo it if you change your mind.

It works in Messages, Notes, Mail, and many other apps where you type.

Knowing you can always back up a step can make typing feel a lot less stressful.
