⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Ever scroll through pages of notes trying to find that one important list or phone number?

The Notes app lets you pin your most-used notes so they stay right at the top, no scrolling required.

Just touch and hold a note from the main list, then tap Pin. It'll stay pinned until you unpin it.

This works well for things like a packing list, emergency contacts, or a running grocery list you update often.
