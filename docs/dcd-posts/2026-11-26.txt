⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Sometimes your eyes are tired, or a message is just long and hard to read.

Your iPhone can read text aloud for you, from emails, articles, or notes.

Go to Settings, then Accessibility, then Spoken Content, and turn on Speak Screen.

Next time you want to listen instead of read, swipe down from the top of the screen with two fingers.

A calm voice will read the page, and you can pause or adjust the speed anytime.
