⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Typing the same website address over and over can feel like a small daily chore.

Bookmarking it means you can skip the typing and the searching altogether.

Open the website in Safari, tap the share icon (the square with an arrow), then choose Add Bookmark.

From then on, that site is just a tap away in your bookmarks list.

It's a small habit that can make your most-used sites feel instantly easier to reach.
