⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Ever scroll way down a long webpage or list, then have to swipe and swipe to get back to the top?

There's a quicker way. Just tap the very top of your screen, right where the time and battery icon are, and the page will jump straight back to the top.

This works in many apps, including Safari, Mail, and Messages, whenever you're scrolled down a long list or page.

It's a small shortcut, but it can save a lot of unnecessary swiping.
