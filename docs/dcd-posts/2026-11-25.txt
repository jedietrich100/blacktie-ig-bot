⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Getting a call or text that says there's a problem with your bank or account can feel urgent.

Scammers count on that urgency, and they often leave a phone number for you to call back.

Instead of using that number, hang up, take a breath, and look up the company's real number yourself through their official website or the back of your card.

Then call that number directly if you want to check on anything.

This one small pause can help you avoid talking to someone pretending to be your bank.
