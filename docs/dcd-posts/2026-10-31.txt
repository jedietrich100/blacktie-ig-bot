⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Ever remember an errand the moment you leave the store, instead of while you're still inside?

The Reminders app can nudge you at the right place, not just the right time.

Open Reminders and create a new reminder, then tap the small info icon next to it.

Turn on “Remind me at a Location” and choose a place, like Home or a store you visit often.

Choose whether you want the alert when you arrive or when you leave, and you're set.
