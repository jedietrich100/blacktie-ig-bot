⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Ever write a reminder and then forget to check it?

A reminder only helps if it reaches you at the right moment, not just whenever you happen to look.

Open the Reminders app and create a new reminder for something you need to do.

Tap the date and time option and choose when you want to be alerted.

Your phone will gently notify you at that exact moment, so the task doesn't quietly slip away.
