⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Reaching for the side button every time just to check the time can get old fast.

Many iPhones have a feature called Raise to Wake that lights up the screen the moment you pick up your phone.

To turn it on, go to Settings, then Display & Brightness, and switch on Raise to Wake.

Now a simple lift can show you the time, date, and any new notifications, no pressing required.
