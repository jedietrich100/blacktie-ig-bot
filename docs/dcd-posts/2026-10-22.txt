⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Squinting at small text on your screen gets old fast.

Your iPhone has a simple slider that can make text appear larger in many apps, right from one screen.

Here’s how: open Settings, tap Display & Brightness, then tap Text Size and drag the slider until words feel easier to read.

You can always slide it back if it feels too big.
