⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Some group texts turn into a nonstop stream of buzzes, especially around holidays or family planning.

Good news: you can quiet just that one conversation, while still being able to check it anytime.

Open the group chat, tap the names at the top, and turn on Hide Alerts (sometimes labeled Notify Me).

The messages still arrive, but your phone stays calm and quiet.
