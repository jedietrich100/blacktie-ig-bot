⚡ TODAY'S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Digging through Contacts every time you need to call someone can feel like extra work you don't need.

The Phone app has a Favorites tab built just for the people you call most.

Open the Phone app, tap Contacts, choose the person you want, then tap "Add to Favorites."

Now that contact shows up right in the Favorites tab, ready for a one-tap call anytime.
