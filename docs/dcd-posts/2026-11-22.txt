⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Ever open Settings and feel lost, unsure which menu holds the thing you need?

You don't have to hunt through every screen. Settings has its own search bar right at the top.

Open the Settings app, tap the search bar, and type a word like "brightness" or "passwords." Your phone will show you exactly where that setting lives.

This small habit can make your phone feel far less confusing, one search at a time.
