⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Ever stare at a screen, unsure what's actually tappable and what's just text?

Your iPhone has a setting that outlines buttons and links so they stand out clearly.

Go to Settings, then Accessibility, then Display & Text Size, and turn on Button Shapes.

Suddenly, tappable items look different from plain text, so you can move through apps with less guessing.

It's a small change that can make everyday apps feel more predictable and less confusing.
