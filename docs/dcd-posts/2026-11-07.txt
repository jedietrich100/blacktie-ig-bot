⚡ TODAY'S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

In an emergency, every minute matters, and your phone may already have a way to help.

The Health app has a Medical ID feature that can display helpful information, like allergies, conditions, or an emergency contact, right from the Lock Screen, even without unlocking the phone.

Open the Health app, tap your profile picture in the top corner, then tap Medical ID and Edit.

Fill in what feels useful to you, then turn on 'Show When Locked' so it's visible if it's ever needed.
