⚡ TODAY'S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Missed calls can feel stressful, especially when you have to replay a voicemail just to catch what was said.

Many iPhones can show a written transcript right under the voicemail, so you can read it at your own pace.

Open your Phone app and tap Voicemail, then tap on any message. Look below it for the text version, and tap play only if you'd like to hear the voice too.

This small habit can make phone messages feel less rushed and easier to understand.
