⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Scammers sometimes pretend to be a family member in trouble, asking for money fast.

A simple safe word can help you pause and check before reacting.

Next time you're with family, agree on one word or phrase only you all know.

If someone calls or texts claiming to be them and asking for urgent help, ask for that word first.

It's a small step that can bring a little more calm to a stressful moment.
