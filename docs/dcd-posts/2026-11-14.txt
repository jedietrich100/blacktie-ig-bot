⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Misplacing your phone somewhere in the house can feel surprisingly stressful, especially if it's on silent.

If you have access to another Apple device, like an iPad or a family member's iPhone, the Find My app can help.

Open Find My, tap Devices, choose your iPhone, and tap Play Sound. Even a phone on silent will chime so you can follow the sound.

This can also help you check your phone's last known location if it's nearby but out of sight.
