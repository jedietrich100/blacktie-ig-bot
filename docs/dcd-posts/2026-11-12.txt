⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Ever open a news article and have to scroll past ads, videos, and pop-ups just to read a few sentences?

Safari has a simple fix built right in.

When you're on an article, look for the small lines icon on the left side of the address bar at the top of the screen.

Tap it, then choose Show Reader View if it appears. The page instantly turns into clean, simple text that's easier to read.

This can work well on many news sites and blogs, though not every webpage offers it.
