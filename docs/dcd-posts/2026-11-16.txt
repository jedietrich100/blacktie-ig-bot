⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Fumbling for a flashlight in a dark room or parking lot can feel like a hassle, especially when your hands are full.

Your iPhone has a flashlight built right into Control Center, and you can turn it on even before unlocking your phone.

From the Lock Screen or almost any screen, swipe down from the top-right corner to open Control Center.

Tap the flashlight icon, and the light turns on right away. Tap it again to turn it off.

It's a small shortcut, but it can save you a few fumbly seconds when you need light fast.
