⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Getting a weird text from an unknown number can feel unsettling, especially when it asks you to click a link.

Many iPhones let you report these messages right from the Messages app, without replying or clicking anything.

Open the suspicious text, look below the message for "Report Junk," and tap it.

Confirming will delete the message and send a report that can help reduce future spam like it.

It’s a small habit that helps you clean up your inbox and feel a bit more in control.
