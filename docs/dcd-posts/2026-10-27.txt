Confusing letters and notices can feel stressful, especially when they're full of technical or legal wording.

Many AI chatbots can help by explaining the same text in plain, everyday language.

Open a chatbot app or website, type or paste in the confusing part, and ask it to explain what it means in simple terms.

You can also ask follow-up questions if something is still unclear.

This can help you feel more confident before replying, calling, or signing anything.
