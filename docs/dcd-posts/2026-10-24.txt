⚡ TODAY'S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Tired of hanging a picture, stepping back, and realizing it's crooked?

Your iPhone already has a level tool hiding inside an app you may not open much.

Open the Measure app, then tap the Level option at the bottom of the screen.

Lay your phone flat against the picture frame, shelf, or whatever you're hanging, and watch the screen.

When the surface is level, the screen often turns green to let you know.

No extra tools, no extra apps, just a steadier eye for your next project.
