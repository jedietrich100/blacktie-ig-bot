⚡ TODAY’S 30-SECOND TECH WIN

Fumbling with a timer while your hands are busy cooking, cleaning, or holding something else is frustrating.

Your iPhone can start a timer just by listening to you, no tapping required.

Activate Siri by holding the side button or saying “Hey Siri,” then say something like “set a timer for 10 minutes.”

You’ll hear a quick confirmation, and the countdown begins right away.

This can be handy for cooking, laundry, medication reminders, or anything else where you need a timer but your hands are occupied.
